# Design references

The requester supplied these four websites for useful ideas. Their homepages were reviewed in the browser on 11 October 2026.

| Reference | Relevant pattern | Church website adaptation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| https://mahhitech.com/ | Prominent headline, paired actions, image-led project showcase | Retained the large motto and visit action; added real church photography and a direct gallery action |
| https://promassltd.com/ | Clear section hierarchy, structured cards, organized resource and contact links | Added a concise church overview and an accessible footer directory |
| https://nissimgrid.com/ | Photography alongside introductory text, service cards, updates, direct contact | Added photography beside the welcome text and cards for church news, encouragement, and prayer |
| https://darsumviv.com/ | Company-at-a-glance information, serif display typography, repeated contact actions | Added church-at-a-glance information using the verified address and supplied vision, with clear directions and visit links |

The church's existing ivory, navy, gold, and serif branding is retained. Corporate content, business claims, statistics, regulatory credentials, and commercial imagery are not part of the church website. The photos come from the church's own public Facebook page, with source links in `GALLERY-SOURCES.md`.

News and messages link to the church's actual Facebook page. Prayer links open an email addressed to airportbc2017@gmail.com with the subject “Prayer request”; the visitor reviews and sends it in their email app. The static website does not store prayer requests or claim to send messages itself.
